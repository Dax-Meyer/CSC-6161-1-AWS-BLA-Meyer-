
Title: AWS Certified AI Practitioner

Purpose of the Activity

The purpose of this activity was to build a foundational understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Generative AI while preparing for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner certification.

The activity covered the major concepts, technologies, AWS services, and responsible AI practices that are relevant to working with AI solutions on AWS.

The goal was to understand how AI and machine learning work at a high level, how Generative AI applications are built, and how AWS services can be used to develop and support AI solutions.

Lessons Learned / Reflection

One of the biggest lessons I learned is that AI is much broader than Generative AI or chatbots. It includes traditional machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, speech technologies, and newer Generative AI technologies.

I also learned how AWS provides different services for different AI use cases. Understanding the purpose of each service is important when deciding which AWS technology is appropriate for a particular problem.

The material on Generative AI also introduced me to concepts such as foundation models, LLMs, embeddings, tokens, prompt engineering, RAG, and fine-tuning. These concepts will be useful as I continue working with Amazon Bedrock and other AI technologies.
